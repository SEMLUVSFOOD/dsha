# Code of conduct

DSHA follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, patient and respectful. Harassment, insults and
personal attacks aren't accepted in issues, pull requests or any other project
space. Assume good intent: many people here are new to DS homebrew or to Home
Assistant.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour is a problem, contact the maintainer privately through
the contact options on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/semluvsfood)
or a private
[security advisory](https://github.com/semluvsfood/dsha/security/advisories/new)
if nothing else works. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may
remove comments, close issues or block people who don't follow this code.
